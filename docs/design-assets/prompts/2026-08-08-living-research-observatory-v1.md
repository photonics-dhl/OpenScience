# Living Research Observatory — MiniMax static-image prompt v1

## Use and constraints

- **Use case:** `stylized-concept`
- **Intended surface:** the cross-surface Figma visual master for OpenScience
- **Composition:** leave usable negative space for native Figma overlays.
- **Native overlays:** product text, RO labels, nodes, charts, metadata, controls and interaction states remain editable Figma/SVG/HTML elements.

## Prompt

Create a restrained, editorial scholarly material field for a Living Research Observatory. Show evidence trajectories moving through an open six-node Research Object structure: six clearly distinct but non-decorative connection points, archival paper fragments, laboratory material textures, subtle research-image crops and traceable version layers. Use deep ink, warm paper, muted blue evidence paths and one restrained orange diff trace. The scene must feel like a contemporary academic publication and precision research instrument, with generous quiet negative space for native Figma overlays. It is a stylized concept image, not a UI mockup, dashboard or data visualization.

## Negative prompt

No product text, logo, watermark, fake readable data, galaxy, brain, glossy gaming HUD, purple gradient, dominant neon, floating generic particles, decorative flower-petal geometry or fake scientific chart.

## Review gate

Reject an output that includes watermarking, readable fabricated content, generic space/brain imagery, excessive neon, malformed six-node geometry or insufficient clear space for native overlays. Do not import an asset into Figma before visual approval.
