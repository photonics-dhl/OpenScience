fix(gateway): make escalateMaxTokens effective across the full retry budget

The previous `maxRetries: 2` on the review path was effectively a
no-op for length-finish cases: the loop did `attempt -= 1; continue;`
right after a successful escalation, which the for-loop's own
`attempt++` immediately undid. Net result: 1 call at the original
budget, then 1 call at the escalated budget, regardless of
`maxRetries`.

The fix: drop the `attempt -= 1;` line. The escalation still happens
once (it must, because repeated escalation does not repair a truncated
response), but now the for-loop's normal `attempt++` gives the
escalated run its own retry slots — `maxRetries: 2` is honoured end to
end (1 normal + 2 escalated = 3 calls total, the third at the original
budget, the first two at the escalated budget).

Also bump the review path's `escalateMaxTokens` from 16384 to 24576.
The science review for a 3-scene brief needs more than 16K to fit the
verdict; 24K is well below the provider's 64K limit and gives the
multi-element briefs a real shot.

For the planner path the same fix is in effect retroactively: the
`completeStructured` loop is shared. Knolling, ink-notes, and any
future multi-element style should now reach a successful plan +
review at least once.
