# Research desk

- Job: help a signed-in researcher resume work or hand material to Hermes.
- First viewport: greeting and current research/action, then Hermes task state, then
  create/import, literature and library.
- Use one dominant continuation surface. Keep creation in the desk; do not repeat
  it in the global public header.
- Desktop is a generous two-column editorial work surface. Mobile becomes a single
  ordered column; Hermes remains a compact expandable companion.
- Prefer rules and whitespace over a grid of equal cards. Use real research titles
  and task states as the visual anchors.
