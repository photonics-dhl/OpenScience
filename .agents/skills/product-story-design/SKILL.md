# Product story and interface craft

Read the current user decisions and the relevant page in [the product design](../../../docs/specs/2026-09-05-integrated-research-product-design.md). This project already has product context: reuse it rather than opening another positioning interview or creating PRODUCT.md / DESIGN.md copies. Deployment and acceptance live in CURRENT, never in this skill.

Treat every surface as a visitor task. Public exploration invites reading; a research desk advances private work; a guide makes the first meaningful action understandable. For a changed area, identify what the reader wants, what the area lets them see or do, and what observable result supports its message. Use these answers to choose the composition, then write copy. A page can have one dominant action without turning every region into a CTA.

For a full-page refinement, read [the UI craft protocol](references/ui-craft-protocol.md) before editing. It is the executable version of the earlier guide-page process: establish the page job, set the first-viewport reading order, reserve the Hermes footprint, then verify the actual route at desktop and narrow widths. Treat Landing as a preserved surface unless the user explicitly changes that scope.

## Narrative decisions

- Start with the researcher's materials, question or discovery. Put the particular benefit beside an actual research artifact or an existing product action. “Continue”, “explore” and “understand” need an object in context.
- Headlines carry motivation; the next sentence adds how the product helps. Instructions belong next to the corresponding action. Repeating the same idea as a label, title, paragraph and helper weakens it.
- Keep scientific prose, source identity and factual claims intact. A public study may demonstrate a layout; it does not become the viewer's private research. Never invent accounts, results, dates, quotations, metrics or a completed Hermes task to decorate a screen.
- Use product rules at the moment they affect a decision. Preserve real permissions, review and recovery behavior; avoid making the introductory story read like a disclaimer or an operations manual.
- Creation starts in the research desk. Public navigation supports exploration and reading; account tools hold settings, developer/API and role-specific management. Check the newest Landing scope before shared navigation or CSS changes.

## Composition decisions

- Let a real contribution, scientific figure or current research action carry the visual anchor. Use the existing optical art and Hermes assets for identity. Do not draw invented scientific relationships or fill a quiet page with stock motifs.
- Preserve the approved cold white / ink / teal, academic editorial and Swiss direction. Use the actual Chinese and English faces already delivered by the app; Latin-only display fonts do not establish a Chinese typographic voice.
- Distinguish invitation, research title, reading copy, controls and metadata by role. Tune line breaks, measure and spacing using real localized content. Containers should express relationships: remove a frame when whitespace or a single rule already does its work.
- A desk needs scanable work and progress; a reading page gives space to the artifact and text. Do not apply a marketing hero, a fixed card count or massive whitespace to every surface.
- Motion expresses a change of task, a selection or immediate feedback. Keep content visible by default, respect reduced motion and preserve the reading focus. A repeated entrance animation on every section is not a product story.

## Learn, test, retain

Use a bounded critique of the same real page to test a new design skill. Record its specific decisions and compare the resulting composition, reading path and usable states. Downloads, detectors, screenshots and reviewer praise alone do not establish product quality.

Useful external methods: [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) for surface modes, visual critique and type/space refinement; [Marketing Skills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills) for audience, benefit and action language. Apply only the relevant references. Existing user decisions outrank their interviews, fixed page formulas, English copy conventions and unsupported marketing statistics. Keep project context in the existing design document.

For visual direction, also consult [Anthropic frontend-design](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/frontend-design): ground the composition in the subject, review the plan against the brief before coding, and concentrate expression where it earns attention. Use [Anthropic design-critique](https://github.com/anthropics/knowledge-work-plugins/tree/main/design/skills/design-critique) for a bounded review of first impression, usability, hierarchy, consistency and accessibility. Declare whether the input is a live page, Figma design, screenshot or source description. Source descriptions can expose structural issues, but cannot establish the first visual impression, actual contrast or interaction quality. A critique must produce specific alternatives for the same researcher task; a skill's popularity or a detector's pass does not choose the design.

Inspect the actual resulting desktop and mobile UI through the supported browser. Test the changed interaction path and long/localized content. If browser access is unavailable, preserve the failure and report source-only findings as such; do not fabricate a rendered result or use another mechanism to bypass the restriction. Keep a candidate distinct from deployed, observed and user-accepted product quality.
